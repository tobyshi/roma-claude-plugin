# Roma

The to-do list that does itself, in every AI chat. Roma gives your AI chat a place to keep things. Ask it to save a plan as a note, add tasks to a list, or fill a list of books, contacts or expenses, and it is still there tomorrow, in this chat or any other. Start a conversation and it can see where you are: what's due today, what you're working on, recent notes, your lists, and what you asked Roma to remember about you.

## Use it

Ask Claude what is on your plate, to save a plan as a note, to add a few books to your reading list, or to pick up a task and work on it. The skill in this plugin tells Claude when to reach for Roma and how to use its tools; the Roma connector it references is where your tasks, notes and lists live. The first time, Claude opens a browser where you sign in to Roma and approve the connection.

## Data

Privacy policy: https://roma.app/privacy

The plugin sends what you ask Claude to save or look up to your own Roma account, over OAuth, as you. It stores nothing itself and reaches nothing but your workspace. The server it talks to is https://api.roma.app/mcp. Developer docs: https://roma.app/developers.
