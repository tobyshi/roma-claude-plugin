# Roma

The to-do list that does itself, in every AI chat. Roma is the to-do list that does itself. Say what's on your mind and Roma gets straight to work. Come back to something real to review, finish, and keep.

## Use it

Ask Claude what is on your plate, to save a plan as a note, to add a few books to your reading list, or to pick up a task and work on it. The skill in this plugin tells Claude when to reach for Roma and how to use its tools; the Roma connector it references is where your tasks, notes, projects and lists live. The first time, Claude opens a browser where you sign in to Roma and approve the connection.

## Data

Privacy policy: https://roma.app/privacy

The plugin sends what you ask Claude to save or look up to your own Roma account, over OAuth, as you. It stores nothing itself and reaches nothing but your workspace. The server it talks to is https://api.roma.app/mcp. Developer docs: https://roma.app/developers.
